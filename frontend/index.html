How IGRIS makes a recommendation

1. Match: your location is matched against 6,635 official assessment units. 2. Context: state resource, water-quality, and seasonal depth indicators are added. 3. Explain: IGRIS converts the category into purpose-aware actions without pretending it is a statutory approval.

Primary source: Central Ground Water Board, Ground Water Resource Assessment 2025, using the GEC-2015 methodology.
